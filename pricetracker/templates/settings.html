Trusted merchants

Only listings from these sellers are accepted. One per line: Name | aliases | domains. A listing is trusted when its link's domain matches (subdomains included), or, when Google hides the merchant link, when the seller name matches the name or an alias. A seller named like a trusted store but linking elsewhere is rejected as a possible impostor. Marketplace sellers ("eBay - someseller") are matched by the part before the dash.
