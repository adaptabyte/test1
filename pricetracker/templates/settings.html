Search sources

{{ error("sources") }}

SearXNG free

Your own SearXNG instance; docker compose up starts one for you. SearXNG has no Google Shopping engine, so it is used two ways: its eBay engine (eBay search results with prices) and web search limited to trusted stores, where the tracker opens each listing page to read its price from the store's product data. Stores with strong bot protection (often Amazon and Walmart) may not give a price; those listings show up as rejected with that reason.

eBay Browse API free recommended

eBay's official API: reliable prices, shipping and condition, plus seller feedback (used to skip risky sellers, see Trusted merchants). Free for up to 5,000 searches a day. Create a free account at developer.ebay.com, create a Production keyset, and paste its App ID (client ID) and Cert ID (client secret). eBay asks new keysets about account-deletion notifications; choosing the exemption for apps that don't store eBay user data is fine for this tracker.

Trusted merchants

Only listings from these sellers are accepted. One per line: Name | aliases | domains. A listing is trusted when its link's domain matches (subdomains included), or, when Google hides the merchant link, when the seller name matches the name or an alias. A seller named like a trusted store but linking elsewhere is rejected as a possible impostor. Marketplace sellers ("eBay - someseller") are matched by the part before the dash.
